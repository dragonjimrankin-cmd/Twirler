Plain-English notes on age restriction, performer records and data handling. This page is a working draft and should be reviewed by a solicitor before launch.

18+ and age assurance

This site is strictly for adults aged 18 or over. An interstitial gate is shown on first visit per browser session. That gate is a courtesy notice, not age assurance: UK providers of online pornography are expected to use age-assurance measures that are technically accurate, robust, reliable and fair under the Online Safety Act 2023.

Before public launch, the studio should add a compliant age-assurance step — a verified payment method, a digital ID or an age-estimation provider — and keep records of the measure used and its configuration.

Performer records

For every performer and every shoot, Twirler retains: photographic ID showing date of birth, a signed model release covering the scene and its promotional use, the stage name used, and the shoot date. Records are retained for at least seven years and are produced on request by a regulator or distributor.

Content standards

All performers are 18 or over. Content is produced with consent, agreed limits and a stop-at-any-time policy. No content depicts minors, non-consensual activity, or anything prohibited under UK law. Footage is withheld from release where consent is withdrawn.

Privacy

This site is static: it sets no advertising cookies and has no analytics installed by default. Age verification state is kept in the browser's session storage and never transmitted. Enquiry form submissions are not stored on the site — the form composes an email in your own client.

Enquiries received by email are kept only as long as needed to answer them, then deleted. Identity documents are never requested by email; a secure upload link is provided instead.
